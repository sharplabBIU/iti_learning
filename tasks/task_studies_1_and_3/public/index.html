Informed Consent

You are invited to participate in a research study about decision-making, which aims to better understand how people learn and plan.

This study is being conducted by researchers at Bar-Ilan University, led by Dr. Paul Sharp.

You will first play an online computer-based decision-making task. This session should take less than 15 minutes, and you may receive a monetary bonus based on your performance in the task.

After completing the task, you will complete a short questionnaire.

Payment rate: You will receive a rate of $9 per hour, calculated based on the estimated time required to complete the study.

By providing your consent to participate, you agree to take part in all parts of the study.

Participation is entirely voluntary. You may choose not to participate, or to stop participating at any time, even after you have agreed, without any negative consequences.

You have the right to withdraw your consent at any point. Data collected prior to withdrawal may still be used for research purposes.

Participation may not provide direct personal benefit; however, the knowledge gained from this study may benefit others in the future.

There are no known risks or discomforts associated with participation in this study.

All responses are fully anonymized. Your Prolific ID will be replaced with a non-identifiable code, and no personally identifying information will be stored with the data.

The data collected will be used for scientific research and securely stored. Research findings may be shared through academic publications, conferences, and data repositories, and may also be used for educational purposes or future research.

By clicking "I Consent" below, you indicate that you have read and understood this consent form, and you agree to participate in this research study.

Instructions

The goal of the game is to win as much money as possible.

Choose between three images using your keyboard.
Each image has a different probability of reward.

Q = Left image
T = Middle image
P = Right image

Instructions

You will receive bonus money based on your performance.
The bonus will be calculated based on 10 random trials from the game.

Once the images appear on the screen, you have 2 seconds to make your choice.

If you do not choose an image within 2 seconds in 5 total trials or more, the game will end.
